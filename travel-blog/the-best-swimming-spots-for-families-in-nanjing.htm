The Best Swimming Spots for Families in Nanjing

  • Home
  • Travel Blog
  • Blog Details

Nanjing, a city steeped in imperial history and cultural grandeur, might first bring to mind majestic walls, serene lakes, and ancient temples. But as the Yangtze River winds its way through and the summer sun beats down, a different side of this dynamic city emerges—one that’s all about cooling off, splashing around, and creating unforgettable family memories. Forget the notion that Nanjing is just a historical destination; it’s also a fantastic place for aquatic adventures perfect for all ages. From crystal-clear water parks to natural lakes with gentle shores, we’ve compiled the ultimate guide to the best swimming spots for families in Nanjing. Get ready to pack your swimsuits, sunscreen, and a sense of fun!

Making a Splash: Modern Water Parks & Pools

When the summer heat hits its peak, sometimes you need the guaranteed thrill and convenience of a modern aquatic facility. Nanjing offers several top-notch options that combine safety, variety, and pure excitement.

Tangshan Water Park: A Geothermal Adventure

Venture a bit outside the city center to Tangshan, famous for its hot springs. But did you know it’s also home to a fantastic water park? This spot uniquely blends the comfort of warm spring-fed pools with the adrenaline rush of water slides. For families, this is a win-win. Parents can relax in therapeutic warm pools while kids and teens conquer colorful slides and splash pads. The water temperature is often more comfortable than chilly regular pools, making it excellent for younger children. It’s a perfect day trip that combines the novelty of Nanjing’s hot spring culture with classic water park fun.

Olympic Sports Center Natatorium: For the Sporty Family

If your family is serious about swimming laps or you prefer a cleaner, less chaotic environment, the pools at the Nanjing Olympic Sports Center are a top choice. This world-class facility offers Olympic-standard lanes for older kids and adults wanting a workout, but it also features dedicated family and learner pools. The water quality is meticulously maintained, and the professional lifeguard presence provides unparalleled peace of mind. It’s less about flashy slides and more about enjoying the pure, refreshing feel of a perfect swim. Consider signing the kids up for a short-term swimming lesson here—it’s a life skill and a great way to beat the heat!

Nature’s Playground: Lakes, Springs & Beaches

Nanjing’s natural beauty provides a stunning backdrop for a more relaxed swimming experience. These spots are about connecting with nature while taking a dip.

The Xuanwu Lake Shoreline: Urban Oasis

The iconic Xuanwu Lake, nestled right in the city center, is a hub of activity. While swimming in the main lake isn’t permitted, head to its designated swimming area, Xuanwu Lake Swimming Pool, near the lakeshore. This cordoned-off section gives you the beautiful views of the lake, Zifeng Tower shimmering in the distance, and the city skyline, all from the safety of a managed pool. It’s incredibly scenic and accessible. After your swim, you can rent a paddle boat, stroll around the park, or visit the nearby Nanjing Museum. It’s a full day of diverse activities in one stunning location.

Gucha Lake in Pukou: A Taste of the Beach

Longing for a beach day without leaving Nanjing? Gucha Lake in the Pukou District is your answer. This vast reservoir has been developed with a beautiful golden-sand artificial beach, often called "Nanjing's Beach." The slope into the water is very gentle, making it supremely safe for toddlers and young kids to wade and play. Build sandcastles, enjoy a picnic on the shore, and take a refreshing swim in the lake’s clean water. The area is family-centric, with vendors selling inflatables and snacks. It’s a vibrant, festive atmosphere that feels like a mini-vacation.

Hotel Havens: Resort-Style Relaxation

For families seeking ultimate convenience, luxury, and safety—especially with very young children—Nanjing’s high-end hotel pools are a hidden gem. This is a growing trend, turning a simple hotel stay into a resort-like aquatic retreat.

The Shangri-La Hotel Nanjing Indoor Pool

A classic choice, the Shangri-La’s pool is a serene, temperature-controlled escape. Floor-to-ceiling windows let in natural light, and the elegant setting makes parents feel pampered. The shallow end is perfect for little ones, and the attentive staff often provide kids' floats. It’s ideal for a relaxing afternoon swim without worrying about weather or crowds.

Jinling Hotel’s Rooftop Oasis

For something truly special, the Jinling Hotel’s rooftop pool offers breathtaking panoramic views of the city. Swimming here feels glamorous and fun. It’s usually quieter than public spots, allowing for quality family time. Many of these hotel pools offer "day pass" options, so you don’t necessarily need to be a guest to enjoy them—always call ahead to check availability and policies.

Pro Tips for a Smooth-Sailing Swim Day in Nanjing

  • Timing is Everything: Weekdays are always less crowded than weekends. For water parks, arrive right at opening time to enjoy popular slides with minimal wait. Late afternoons on weekdays can also be a good bet.
  • Sun & Safety Arsenal: Nanjing’s summer sun is intense. Pack high-SPF, waterproof sunscreen, reapplying often. Rash guards for kids are highly recommended. Don’t forget swim diapers for babies, waterproof phone cases, and plenty of hydration.
  • Snack Smart: While most parks and natural spots have vendors, prices can be high, and options might not suit picky eaters. Pack a cooler with fruits, sandwiches, and plenty of water bottles. Many scenic spots are perfect for a family picnic.
  • Embrace the Post-Swim Culture: After your swim, dive into Nanjing’s famous food scene. Nothing beats a family meal after a day in the water. Seek out local favorites like salted duck, tangbao (soup dumplings), or a refreshing bowl of lengmian (cold noodles). The Xinjiekou or Confucius Temple areas offer endless family-friendly dining options.
  • Check Ahead: Always verify opening hours and specific rules before you go, especially post-holidays or during inclement weather. Some facilities may have maintenance closures.

The joy of a family swim day isn’t just about getting wet; it’s about the shared laughter, the races, the floating conversations, and the blissful exhaustion at the end of the day. Nanjing, with its harmonious blend of the modern and the natural, offers a wonderful array of settings for exactly this kind of joy. So, map out your aquatic adventure, grab your towels, and discover why Nanjing is a fantastic destination for making a splash with your loved ones.
